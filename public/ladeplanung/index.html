Load planning for event and AV gear — computed for the cargo space you actually have, not for a box.

What is different here

  • Vans, not only semi-trailers. Wheel arches, rounded roof edges, the rear door opening: the cargo space is not a box, and the packer works with the one that is there.
  • Cases the way they travel. Castors, castor dishes in the lid, allowed orientations (upright, on its side, on end), stacking limits — not bare boxes.
  • A printable axle-load plan. Centre of gravity and axle loads from the placed positions, plus a plan per layer, a dock checklist and case labels.
  • The stock knows what is inside each case. If case 7 is missing, so is the spare wireless kit inside it — because load planning is part of the warehouse and does not start from a spreadsheet.
